TSLA: Tesla Stock Dashes Through the Charts as Traders Rush to Buy the Dip

Key points:
  • Tesla shares rally 7.4%
  • EV maker’s stock stages recovery
  • Honda-Nissan merger doesn’t faze traders

  • Tesla stock powered higher by 7.4% on Christmas Eve as bargain-hungry traders rushed to buy the dip after several days of the shares languishing. The EV maker became the S&P 500’s top performer for the day and recouped a big chunk of its recent losses. Following the Federal Reserve’s message of a slowdown in the interest rate cuts, Tesla lost about 13% of its record high valuation hit last week.

  • The 7.4% bump in Tesla’s shares lifted the company’s valuation to $1.45 trillion, some $50 billion shy of the ultimate record high. Now, the EV maker boasts year-to-date gains of 86%. But even more impressive is that Elon Musk’s company is up 90% since Election Day, November 5, in no small part thanks to Donald Trump’s ties with the Tesla CEO — the two have big ideas moving forward (if their partnership doesn’t crumble under a battle of the egos).

  • In other news, Tesla didn’t show any reaction to the announcement of Honda and Nissan merging some time next year. The move is expected to boost competition for affordable car sales as the new Japanese group would potentially become the world’s third-largest car manufacturer by sales. There’s also looming competition from China where the EV market is a highly popular corner of the car industry.
