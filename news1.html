XAU/USD: Gold Picks Up Quiet Gains as Post-Christmas Buying Lifts Prices to $2,630

Key points:
  • Gold prices quietly move higher
  • Traders go technical on bullion
  • Major moving averages in play

  • Gold prices XAUUSD :perked up early Thursday as traders were showing some appetite for safe-haven plays. In contrast, futures on the S&P 500 stayed silent with no trading momentum to stir dealmaking in the stock corner. It’s the final trades of the year, and by the looks of it, a good chunk of the volume goes to bullion — prices jumped about 0.5% to approach $2,630, on track for the second day of gains in a row.

  • But similar to equity futures, gold is seeing some quietness after a tumultuous year is about to wrap up. Stubborn inflation, war tremors and geopolitical tensions with China are now mostly priced in and traders are turning to the art (or is it science?) of technical analysis. And as usual, there’s always something to dig into and this time it’s the moving average trends. Let’s take a look at the three main moving average indicators and what they mean.

  • First, the 200-day moving average, tracking the average price over the past 200 days, is well below current market prices at $2,480. That’s a strong bullish sign. The next one — the 100-day line — is right at current market prices, indicating buyers and sellers are still trying to figure out where to head next. A definite break above it could push prices to the 50-day moving average, resting at $2,670 per ounce.
